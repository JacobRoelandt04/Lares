# The sheet check's reference sheet: every state it renders, by a name that outlives the moves (tools/dev/sheet_render.py reads this;
# `python3 tools/dev/sheet_render.py --list` prints the expanded names). One row per line:
#
#   shell  <name>  [VAR=VALUE ...] [cfg=user] [scales=<k,k>] [modes=<m,m>] [grab=<ms>] [mask=<name>[@<k>]:<x>,<y>,<w>,<h>:<reason with _ for blanks>]
#   mockup <moduleId> <file> [the same]
#
# A shell row is mockups/shell.qml (the mock services on the shell's real ShellFrame, one 2880 x 1620 output over a fixed wallpaper), once per
# mode and per global scale: shell.<name>.<mode>.<k>x. A mockup row is that mockup's stage at zoom 1, its panel left out, once per mode:
# mockup.<moduleId>.<mode>.
#
# cfg=user: the mock config dir holds tools/dev/sheet_user_shell.jsonc, the non-default values the user's own desktop runs on.
# grab=<ms>: GRAB_MS for this state (6000 by default; a QML Timer's interval, on the animation clock).
# mask: a rectangle left out of the comparison. Only for what cannot be frozen or staged; the reason says why. Two rows have one: at a
# global scale of 1.25 a glyph edge reads one level apart now and then (CLAUDE.md, TEXT AT A FRACTIONAL x: which glyphs sit side by
# side in Qt's glyph atlas follows the order they were first drawn, and at a fractional scale the sampling takes in its neighbour).
# Seen in 3 of 9 runs, always these glyphs: the left edges of the audio rows' "80 %" and the wiki card's W mark. A mask names the scale
# it is for as `<name>@<k>` (1.25 when none is named): round 9 found the W mark one level off at 1x in dark mode too, on an unchanged tree
# against its own baseline (one pixel, 2228,310), so the wiki row has a 1x mask of its own.
