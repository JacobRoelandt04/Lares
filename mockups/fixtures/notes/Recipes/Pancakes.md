# Pancakes

![A stack of pancakes](../.attachments/pancakes.png)

Serves 4. Rest the batter for 20 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 250 g flour
- 2 eggs
- 500 ml milk
- a pinch of salt
- butter for the pan

## Method

1. Whisk flour and salt, make a well.
2. Add the eggs, then the milk bit by bit.
3. Bake on a hot, buttered pan, about a minute a side.
